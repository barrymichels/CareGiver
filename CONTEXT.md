# Caregiving schedule

The family coordinates caregiving visits through weekly schedules.

## Language

**Schedule week**: A Monday-through-Sunday period with its own saved visit times.

**Schedule template**: A reusable set of visit times and labels for each weekday. Applying a template saves a copy of those times in one schedule week.

**Default template**: The template used to initialize a week without a saved schedule. Changing it does not change weeks that already have saved schedules.

**Timeslot**: A visit time on a particular day of a schedule week.

**Availability**: A caregiver's indication of whether they can cover a particular timeslot.

**Assignment**: The caregiver selected to cover a particular timeslot.
